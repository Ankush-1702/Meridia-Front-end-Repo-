# How to download your exports

Find exports you requested and download them as CSV files.

## Before you begin
- Request an export from a port, zone, vessel or fleet page first **[CONFIRM where export is requested]**.
- Exports are available to download for 24 hours after they're generated.

## Steps
1. Click **Notifications** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Downloads** tab in the panel to see recent exports. Alternatively, click **View All** and then the **Downloads** tab on the Notifications page.
   `[SCREENSHOT 1: Downloads tab in the Notifications panel]`
3. On the **Downloads** tab, find your export. Columns are **Export**, **Type**, **Requested On**, **Status** and **Expires**.
   `[SCREENSHOT 2: Downloads table]`
4. Check the **Status**: **Processing**, **Completed**, **Failed** or **Expired**.
5. When it is **Completed**, click **Download CSV**.
6. A message "CSV downloaded successfully!" appears.
7. To narrow the list, open **Type** and tick **Port**, **Zone**, **Vessel** or **Fleet**.
8. To search, type in **Search exports...**.
9. Click **Refresh** (next to "Updated ... ago") to update the list.

## Expected result
The CSV file downloads to your computer.

## Good to know
- **Expires** counts down, for example "Expires in 3h".
- Failed exports show **Retry**. Expired exports show **Request again**.
- The panel only lists exports requested recently in this browser. The page lists recent exports **[CONFIRM how many]**.
- The panel shows **No downloads available** if there are none.
- If an export is still running, a message says "Export is still processing...".

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Status is **Failed** | Click **Retry**. The panel shows "Export failed — please try again." |
| Status is **Expired** | Click **Request again**. |
| A message says "Export completed but download URL is missing" | Click **Refresh**, then try again. |
