# How to turn notifications on for a port or zone

Choose which events trigger notifications, and whether you also get emails.

## Before you begin
- The notification settings button is on **port** and **zone** pages. It is not shown on vessel or fleet pages.
- Turning notifications on adds the item to your library if it is not already there.

## Steps
1. Open a port or zone page.
2. Click the bell-with-gear button labelled **Manage Notifications** in the top toolbar. It is orange when notifications are on and grey when off.
   `[SCREENSHOT 1: Manage Notifications button in the toolbar]`
3. Turn on **Enable Live Notifications**.
   - If the item is not in your library, a message says "This item will be added to your library if notifications are enabled".
   `[SCREENSHOT 2: Settings panel with Enable Live Notifications on]`
4. Turn on the events you want.
   - Ports: **Area Arrivals**, **Arrivals**, **Departures**, **Area Departures**.
   - Zones: **Entries**, **Exits**.
5. To screen vessels automatically, turn on **PurpleTRAC Auto-Screening**. It appears under **Area Arrivals** (ports) or **Entries** (zones) once that event is on.
6. Under **SEVERITY**, turn on the levels you want: **Critical**, **Warning**, **OK**, **Unscored**.
   `[SCREENSHOT 3: PurpleTRAC Auto-Screening and Severity toggles]`
7. To get emails, turn on **Email Notifications**.
8. Next to **Timing**, choose **Immediately** or **Scheduled**.
9. If you chose **Scheduled**, pick **Emails per day** (**1**, **2**, **3**, **4** or **Hourly**).
10. Set **Send at** to the time of day the first email is sent.
   `[SCREENSHOT 4: Email Notifications, Timing and Send at]`

## Expected result
The button turns orange and your changes save automatically after a moment. There is no Save button.

## Good to know
- Turning **Enable Live Notifications** on switches on all events for that item **[CONFIRM defaults]**. Turning it off switches off all events and email.
- If you switch off all port events, or both zone events, **Enable Live Notifications** turns off.
- Turning **PurpleTRAC Auto-Screening** on selects all four severity levels. Severity applies to email alerts, and only when PurpleTRAC Auto-Screening is on.
- **Immediately** on large zones and ports may trigger a large amount of emails.
- **Send at** is the first email time; later emails follow the **Emails per day** interval. It is shown in your local time.
- If your account does not include PurpleTRAC Auto-Screening, it shows a lock icon and a greyed-out toggle. Hover the info icon for "Contact your account manager to enable PurpleTRAC Auto-Screening for compliance and sanctions risks."
- Which accounts see the lock vs. the toggle depends on account permissions **[CONFIRM]**.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I cannot find the button | Make sure you are on a port or zone page, not a vessel or fleet page. |
| PurpleTRAC Auto-Screening is greyed out | Contact your account manager. |
| I do not see PurpleTRAC Auto-Screening | Turn on **Area Arrivals** or **Entries** first. |
| Nothing appears in Notifications | Check **Enable Live Notifications** is on and an event is selected. |
