# How to edit, rename or delete a fleet

Change a fleet's name, description or vessels, or delete it, from the Library or the fleet page.

> Available only if fleet management is enabled for your account.

## Steps: edit or rename
1. In the Library, click the **Fleets** tab.
2. Hover over a fleet card and click the **More actions** button at the top right.
   `[SCREENSHOT 1: Fleet card menu with Open, Edit and Delete]`
3. Click **Edit**. (From a fleet page, click the menu beside the fleet name and click **Edit**.)
   `[SCREENSHOT 2: Fleet page menu with Edit and Delete]`
4. On the **Edit** page, change **Name** or **Description (optional)** to rename the fleet or update its note.
5. To add vessels, use **Search vessel name or IMO...** or the **Bulk Import** tab, as when you create a fleet.
6. To remove vessels, click the X beside a vessel in the **Vessels** panel, or use **Clear fleet**.
   `[SCREENSHOT 3: Edit page with the Vessels panel]`
7. Click **Save Changes**.

## Steps: delete
1. Open the fleet's menu as above (Library card or fleet page) and click **Delete**.
2. In **Delete fleet?**, read what will be removed.
   `[SCREENSHOT 4: Delete fleet? confirmation]`
3. Click **Delete fleet** to confirm.

## Expected result
- After saving, "Fleet "name" updated successfully" appears. You return to the fleet page if you came from it, otherwise to the Library **Fleets** tab.
- After deleting, "Fleet deleted successfully" appears and you return to the Library **Fleets** tab.

## Good to know
- **Save Changes** stays greyed out until you change something. The name rules are the same as when creating a fleet.
- **Cancel** always asks you to confirm with **Discard changes?** while editing. Click **Discard changes** to leave or **Keep editing** to stay.
- Deleting removes the fleet and its vessel list, stops its notifications and alerts, and removes its analytics and event history. The vessels stay in Meridia and in other fleets. **This can't be undone.**
- The delete window shows the fleet's name, type and vessel count.
- Editing a fleet can't be done from the list view's rows; use the cards. **[CONFIRM]** whether list view offers fleet actions.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No **Edit** or **Delete** option | Your account may not have fleet management; contact your administrator. **[CONFIRM]** |
| "A fleet with this name already exists" | Pick a different name. |
| "Name must be at least 3 characters" | Use a longer name. |
| "Add at least one vessel" | A fleet can't be saved empty; delete it instead if you no longer need it. |
| The edit page shows an error instead of the fleet | Reload the page or return to the Library and try again. |
