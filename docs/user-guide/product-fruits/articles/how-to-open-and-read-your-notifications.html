See new vessel, port and zone notifications from the bell in the sidebar, from pop-ups, or on the full Notifications page.

Before you begin

  • The Notifications entry only appears in the sidebar if your account includes Meridia.
  • You only receive notifications for ports and zones where you have turned notifications on. See "How to turn notifications on for a port or zone".

Steps

  1. Look for the bell icon labelled Notifications in the sidebar.
    • When the sidebar is collapsed, a small dot on the bell means there are new notifications or downloads.
    • When the sidebar is expanded, a number appears beside Notifications (it shows 9+ for more than nine).

    📷 Screenshot 1: Sidebar with the Notifications bell and badge

  2. Click Notifications to open the notifications panel.
  3. On the Notifications tab, read the list. Newest items appear first.

    📷 Screenshot 2: Notifications panel with a list of items

  4. Read each item: a coloured tag, the port or zone name, then the vessel name, the event (for example Arrival or Entry) and how long ago it happened.
  5. To remove one item from the panel list, click the x on that item.
  6. To see everything, click View All at the bottom of the panel.

    📷 Screenshot 3: View All button at the bottom of the panel

  7. You can also click any item in the panel to open the Notifications page.
  8. To close the panel without opening the page, click the x at the top right of the panel.

Expected result

The Notifications page opens at /analytics/notifications and lists your notifications. Opening it clears the bell number or dot and empties the panel list.

Good to know

  • If there is nothing new, the panel shows No notifications.
  • The tag on each item shows the vessel's risk result. It shows NA for exits, port arrivals, port departures, port area departures, and for tug and push-boat vessels, and Unscored if no result is available yet.
  • Removing an item with the x only removes it from the panel list. It does not delete it from the Notifications page [CONFIRM].
  • The panel does not have read or unread states, or a mark-as-read option.
  • If pop-ups are on, new notifications also appear in the bottom-left corner (see "How to turn notification pop-ups on or off").
  • Click a pop-up to open the Notifications page. Click x on a pop-up to close it. When more than one pop-up shows, Dismiss All closes them all.
