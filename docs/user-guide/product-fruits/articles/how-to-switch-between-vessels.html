Change to another vessel without going back to the Library.

Before you begin

  • Open any vessel page first. See "How to open and read a vessel page".

Steps

  1. Select the vessel name at the top of the page, next to the up-and-down arrows icon.

    📷 Screenshot 1: Vessel name button in the page header

  2. In the box that opens, the Recent tab is shown first. Select a vessel from the list to open it.

    📷 Screenshot 2: Switcher open with the Recent tab

  3. To find a vessel not in the list, type in the Search vessels… box. Enter at least two characters of a name or IMO number.
  4. Wait about one second. Matching vessels appear under Results.
  5. Select a result to open that vessel.

    📷 Screenshot 3: Search results under Results

  6. To pick from your saved vessels, select the Library tab.
  7. Type in the Filter saved… box to narrow the list, then select a vessel.

    📷 Screenshot 4: Library tab with saved vessels

Expected result

The page opens the selected vessel, and the map and replay start fresh for it.

Good to know

  • Each entry shows a flag, the vessel name, the vessel type and its IMO number.
  • Recent keeps your last 6 vessels, most recent first. The list is stored in your browser, so it is not shared across browsers or devices.
  • The vessel you are viewing is highlighted in Recent and Library, and is left out of search results.
  • The Library tab shows up to 20 vessels you have added to your Library. [CONFIRM] whether vessels beyond 20 are expected to be listed.
  • Press Esc or click outside the box to close it without switching.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"No recent vessels"Open a few vessels first; they are added automatically.
"No vessels found"Check the spelling or try the IMO number.
"No saved vessels"Add vessels to your Library with Add to Library from the three-dot menu in the header.
No results appear while typingEnter at least two characters and wait a moment.
