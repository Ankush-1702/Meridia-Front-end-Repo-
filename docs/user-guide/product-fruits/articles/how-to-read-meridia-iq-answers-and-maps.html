Understand the text, map, chart and MTI score displays that can appear in a Meridia IQ answer.

Before you begin

  • Send a question in a chat first. See "How to start a chat with Meridia IQ".

Steps

  1. Read the text answer. It can include headings, lists, bold text and tables.

    📷 Screenshot 1: Answer with text and a table

  2. If the answer includes a map, find the bar labelled Map Visualization.
  3. Click the arrows icon (tooltip Expand) to enlarge the map. Click it again (Collapse), or click outside the map, to shrink it.

    📷 Screenshot 2: Embedded map with Legend panel at top left

  4. Click the arrow next to Legend (top left of the map) to open or close it.
  5. Under Positions, choose Blended, Separate or None.
  6. Under Events, tick or untick event types, or use Show/Hide All. Items include Zone Entry, Zone Exit, Port Arrival, Port Departure, Positional Discrepancy, AIS Gap Start, AIS Gap End, STS Pairings and AIS Gap Track.

    📷 Screenshot 3: Legend open with Positions and Events options

  7. Hover over a vessel marker to see its details (for example Name, IMO, MMSI, Flag, Reported Dest, Reported ETA, Speed/Course).
  8. Click a vessel marker to open that vessel's page.
  9. Click a port marker to zoom in. A number on the marker shows how many berths it has. Hover over a port or berth to see its details.
  10. If the answer includes a chart or table, find the bar labelled Chart/Table Visualization and use the same expand arrows.

    📷 Screenshot 4: Chart/Table Visualization panel

  11. For MTI scores of one vessel, read the gauge and the Vessel Score, Reporting Score and Voyages Score badges.
  12. For several vessels, read the table with columns IMO, Rating, MTI Score, Vessel, Reporting and Voyages.
    • Click a column heading to sort. Click it again to reverse the order.
    • Click Next → or ← Prev to change page (5 vessels per page).

    📷 Screenshot 5: MTI score table with sorting arrows and paging

Good to know

  • Score cells are green, amber or red. Colour thresholds seen in the app: 3.5 and above is green, 2.0 and above is amber, below 2.0 is red. [CONFIRM: meaning of the scores and colours]
  • A dash (—) means no value. "MTI data unavailable for this vessel" means no score came back.
  • Markers hovered show "Click for more details". Not every marker opens a page; vessel markers do (when they have an IMO).
  • Meaning of each event type and marker colour. [CONFIRM]
  • There are no like/dislike, copy or share buttons on answers.
  • Accuracy of answers. [CONFIRM] The app itself advises: "We recommend verifying key information."
