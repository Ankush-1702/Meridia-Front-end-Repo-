Use the company details window to see the website, address, phone numbers, departments, contacts and sources found for a company.

Before you begin

  • Run a search from a vessel's Ownership section (see "How to search for company details from a vessel page").
  • Every section appears only if information was found for it.

Steps

  1. Read the banner at the top: "This AI contact search feature is currently in beta - results may be incomplete or inaccurate."

    📷 Screenshot 1: Company details window with Beta banner

  2. Check the heading. It shows the company name and a line such as "Operator for <vessel name> (IMO ...)".
  3. Look at the confidence badge under the heading: High Confidence, Medium Confidence or Low Confidence.
  4. Read the contact block: website (opens in a new tab), headquarters address with a small map, and main phone number.

    📷 Screenshot 2: Website, address, map and phone

  5. Read the blue box, if shown. It can contain Match Type, Warning: Shell Company / SPV and Parent Company.
  6. Read Departments & Contacts. Each card shows the department name, its relevance to maritime, a phone number and an email. A card without details says "No direct contact details."
  7. Read Alternative Contacts. Each card shows the type, location, address, phone and email.
  8. Open Sources to see where the information came from. They are grouped as Tier 1 - Official Sources, Tier 2 - Verified Directories, Tier 3 - Secondary Sources and Tier 4 - Other Sources. Select a source to open it in a new tab.
  9. Read Confidence Assessment for the reason behind the confidence level.
  10. Read AI-Generated Content Disclaimer at the bottom and verify key details before acting on them.

Good to know

  • To search again, select Refresh. It shows "Refreshing..." while it runs.
  • To save the contacts, select Export and choose vCard (.vcf) or CSV (.csv). The CSV has the columns Type, Name, Phone, Email and Website.
  • Export is hidden when no results were found.
  • The map is hidden if the address cannot be located.
  • This window does not list the company's other vessels or fleet.
  • Email addresses open your mail program when selected.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"No reliable results found for this company" and "Please verify via official sources"The search found nothing dependable. Try Refresh, or check the company's official sources.
No Export buttonExport is available only when results exist.
No map under the addressThe address could not be located. Use the address text.
Results look wrongCheck Confidence Assessment and Sources, then verify with the company.
