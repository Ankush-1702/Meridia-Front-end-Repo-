Reset a forgotten password using a one-time code sent to your email.

Before you begin

  • You need access to the email inbox registered with your Meridia account.
  • Plan to finish within 10 minutes – the code expires after that.

Steps

Part 1 – Request a code

  1. On the login page, click Forgotten Password? (below the Sign in button).

    📷 Screenshot 1: Login page with "Forgotten Password?" link highlighted

  2. On Reset your password, type your registered email in Email Address.
    • The Continue button stays disabled until the address is valid ("Invalid email address" appears if not).

    📷 Screenshot 2: Reset your password – email entry

  3. Click Continue. A message "OTP sent successfully" appears and you move to the next screen.

Part 2 – Verify and set a new password

  1. Open the email and copy the 6-digit code.
  2. On Verify your identity, enter the code in Verification Code.

    📷 Screenshot 3: Verify your identity – code and password fields

  3. In New Password, enter your new password. Hover the (i) icon to see the requirements:
    • At least 8 characters
    • At least 1 number
    • At least 1 special character
    • At least 1 uppercase letter
    • At least 1 lowercase letter

    📷 Screenshot 4: Password requirements tooltip

  4. In Confirm New Password, re-enter the same password.
  5. Click Continue.
  6. You return to the login page with "Password reset successfully". Sign in with your new password (see How to log in).

    📷 Screenshot 5: Login page with success message

Expected result

Your password is changed and you can sign in with the new one.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"Code invalid. Please try again or Send a new code"Check the 6 digits or click Send a new code.
"Code expired."Click Send a new code, then use the newest email.
"OTP already sent"A code was requested in the last 10 minutes. Use that email; a new one can be requested after 10 minutes.
"Password invalid. Password requirements: …"Update the password to meet all five rules.
"This Password does not match."Make Confirm New Password identical to New Password.
Continue button greyed outComplete every field correctly.
No email arrivesCheck spam/junk; confirm you used your registered email.
Want to leaveClick Back to Sign in.
