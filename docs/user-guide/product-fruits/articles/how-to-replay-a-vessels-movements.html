Play back the vessel's journey over your chosen dates and see events as they happen.

Before you begin

  • Open the vessel's Overview tab and choose a date range. See "How to view a vessel's track on the map".
  • The replay bar is only active when there are events in the period.

Steps

  1. Find the replay bar under the map.

    📷 Screenshot 1: Replay bar under the map

  2. Select Play to start the replay. The vessel marker moves along the track.
  3. Select Pause to stop.
  4. Select a speed: 1×, 2×, 5× or 10×.
  5. Drag the slider to jump to any time. The current date and time show beside the controls.

    📷 Screenshot 2: Slider with event tick marks

  6. Select Skip to start or Skip to end to go to either end.
  7. Select Follow vessel to keep the map centred on the vessel.
  8. Select Pause at events so the replay stops when it reaches an event.
  9. When it pauses, read the event card beside the Legend. It lists the event type, description and time.

    📷 Screenshot 3: Event card during a replay pause

  10. Select Pause countdown to hold the card open, or Dismiss to close it and continue.
  11. Select Show detailed timeline to open a timeline under the bar. Select Hide detailed timeline to close it.

Expected result

The replay runs through the period and, with Pause at events on, stops briefly at each event.

Good to know

  • Small marks on the slider show events. Marks turn bolder once passed.
  • The event card closes by itself after about 4.5 seconds and the replay resumes.
  • Keyboard: Space plays or pauses, Left and Right arrows step, Home and End jump to start or end, Esc resets.
  • Only event types ticked in the Legend pause the replay.
  • Dragging the slider pauses playback. [CONFIRM] whether the replay restarts from the dragged point.
  • Changing the date range resets the replay.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Controls are greyed outThere are no events in the period. Choose a longer date range.
Replay does not stop at eventsTurn on Pause at events and tick event types in the Legend.
Replay seems stuckSelect Play again, or Skip to start.
