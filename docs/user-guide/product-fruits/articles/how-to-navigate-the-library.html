The Library is your Meridia home page. It holds the vessels, ports, zones and (if enabled for you) fleets you have saved, so you can open them in one click.

Steps

  1. Sign in. Meridia opens the Library (/analytics/library).

    📷 Screenshot 1: Library page – header, tabs, search box, item count, List/Grid switch

  2. Use the tabs under the header to switch between Vessels, Ports and Zones. A Fleets tab also appears if fleet management is enabled for your account.
  3. Check the item count next to the search box (for example "12 items").
  4. Click any card or row to open that vessel, port, zone or fleet.

Good to know

  • The Library only shows items you have added. A new account shows No items – Add items to your library to get started with a + Add items button. See How to add a vessel, port or zone to your Library.
  • If you can't see the Library after login, your account may not include it – you will land on My Profile instead. Contact your account administrator. [CONFIRM wording]
  • The selected tab is kept in the page address (?tab=Vessels), so you can bookmark a tab.
