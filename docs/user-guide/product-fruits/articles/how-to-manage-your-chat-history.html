Reopen, review and delete your past Meridia IQ chats from the sidebar.

Before you begin

  • Expand the left sidebar. The chat list is hidden when the sidebar is minimised.

Steps

  1. In the left sidebar, scroll to the Meridia IQ section. Your chats are listed under it.

    📷 Screenshot 1: Sidebar chat list grouped by Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, Older

  2. Find the chat under its group: Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days or Older.
    • Click a group name to collapse or expand it.
  3. Click a chat title to open it. The earlier messages load, then you can continue with Reply....
  4. To delete a chat, hover over it and click the ... icon (Chat options).

    📷 Screenshot 2: Chat options menu showing Delete

  5. Click Delete.
  6. In the Delete Chat? window, click Delete to confirm, or Cancel to keep it.

    📷 Screenshot 3: Delete Chat? confirmation

  7. To start a fresh chat at any time, click New Chat above the list.

Expected result

A message confirms that the chat was "deleted successfully" and it disappears from the list. If you deleted the chat you were viewing, the next chat in the list opens, or a new empty chat if none remain.

Good to know

  • Chats are named after your first message. Long titles are shortened with "...".
  • Each chat has its own web address ending in the chat's ID. You can bookmark it to return to the chat later. [CONFIRM: whether others can open a shared link]
  • Deleting cannot be undone.
  • There is no search, rename or pin for chats.
  • The list loads the 20 most recent chats. [CONFIRM: how to reach older chats]
