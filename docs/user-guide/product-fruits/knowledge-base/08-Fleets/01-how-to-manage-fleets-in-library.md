# How to open, edit or delete a fleet from the Library

> Available only if fleet management is enabled for your account (you will see a **Fleets** tab).

## Open a fleet
1. In the Library, click the **Fleets** tab.
   `[SCREENSHOT 1: Fleets tab – fleet cards with vessel counts]`
2. Click a fleet card.

## Create a fleet
1. Click **Add** → **Fleet** (under **Create new**), or click **+ Add items** on an empty Fleets tab.

## Edit or delete a fleet
1. Hover over a fleet card and click the **⋮** (More actions) button at the top right.
   `[SCREENSHOT 2: Fleet card menu – Open, Edit, Delete]`
2. Choose **Edit** to change the fleet, or **Delete**.
3. For delete, read the **Delete fleet?** window and click **Delete fleet** (or cancel). A "Fleet deleted successfully" message appears.

## Good to know
Deleting a fleet removes its definition and vessel list, stops its notifications and alerts, and removes its analytics and event history. The vessels themselves are not affected. **This can't be undone.**
