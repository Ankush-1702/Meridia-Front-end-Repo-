# How to change the time zone and theme

Choose how dates and times are shown, and whether Meridia uses light or dark mode.

## Steps
1. Open **My Profile** (click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **My Profile**).
2. In the **General** section, under **Time Zone**, choose:
   - **System** – times follow your device's time zone.
   - **UTC** – all times are shown in UTC.
   `[SCREENSHOT 1: General section – Time Zone and Theme options]`
3. Under **Theme**, choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. The page previews your choice immediately.
4. Click **Save Changes** to keep your choices.
   - To undo unsaved changes, click **Reset**.

## Good to know
- **Save Changes** and **Reset** are greyed out until you change something.
- If you leave the page without clicking **Save Changes**, the theme goes back to your last saved setting.
- **System** theme follows your device's light/dark setting.
