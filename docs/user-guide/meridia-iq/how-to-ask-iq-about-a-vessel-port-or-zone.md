# How to ask Meridia IQ about a vessel, port or zone

Use an **Ask IQ** button to open Meridia IQ and send a ready-made question about the item you are viewing.

## Before you begin
- **Ask IQ** buttons appear only if your account has access to Meridia IQ.

## Steps
1. Open the Library search window (the **Search** item in the sidebar, or the search on the **Library** page).
2. Choose the **Vessels**, **Ports** or **Zones** tab and search for the item.
3. In the result row, click **Ask IQ** (next to **Add to Library**).
   `[SCREENSHOT 1: Search result row with Ask IQ and Add to Library buttons]`
4. Meridia IQ opens and the question is sent automatically. Wait for the answer.
   `[SCREENSHOT 2: Meridia IQ chat showing the sent question and Thinking...]`

Other places to click **Ask IQ**:
5. On a vessel, port or zone page, click **Ask IQ** at the top right of the tabs bar.
   `[SCREENSHOT 3: Vessel page with Ask IQ button]`
6. On a fleet page, open a vessel, port or zone side panel and click **Ask IQ** in the panel header.

## Expected result
A new chat opens with one of these questions already sent:
- Vessel: "Summarise [name] (IMO: [number]) over the last 30 days: current voyage status, port calls, zone events, AIS gaps, and anything else that is notable."
- Port: "Show me the activity for port [name] in the last 24 hours."
- Zone: "Show me the activity for zone [name] in the last 24 hours."

## Good to know
- The question is sent straight away. You cannot edit it first. Ask a follow-up in the same chat to refine it.
- The question uses up part of your daily token limit.
- Ask IQ is not available on the fleet page itself or the fleet event panel.
- Quality and coverage of these summaries. **[CONFIRM]**

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No **Ask IQ** button | Your account may not have Meridia IQ access. Contact your administrator. |
| Nothing is sent | Wait a few seconds; the chat sends once the connection is ready. If you see "Connection not ready. Please try again shortly.", ask again. |
| "Your daily token quota has been reached..." | Wait until the reset time shown. |
