# How to sign out of Meridia

## Steps
1. At the bottom of the left sidebar, click your **name**.
   `[SCREENSHOT 1: User menu with Sign Out]`
2. Click **Sign Out**. "Logging out..." appears and you return to the login page.

## Good to know
- Meridia also signs you out automatically after **60 minutes of inactivity** unless you ticked **Keep me signed in** at login.
- Use Sign Out on shared computers.
