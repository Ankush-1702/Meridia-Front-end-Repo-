# How to log in to Meridia

Sign in to Meridia with your email address and password.

## Before you begin
- You need a Meridia account (email address and password).
- If you have forgotten your password, see **How to reset your password**.

## Steps
1. Open the Meridia login page in your browser.
   `[SCREENSHOT 1: Login page – Meridia logo, Email, Password, "Keep me signed in", Sign in]`
2. In the **Email** field, type your email address.
3. In the **Password** field, type your password.
   `[SCREENSHOT 2: Login form filled in (blur the email)]`
4. *(Optional)* Tick **Keep me signed in** to stay signed in on this device. Leave it unticked on shared computers.
5. Click **Sign in**. The button reads "Signing in..." while your details are checked.
   - The **Sign in** button stays disabled until both Email and Password are filled in.
6. **If your account has two-step verification (MFA)**, the **Verify your identity** screen appears. Continue with **How to log in with a verification code**. Otherwise go to step 7.
7. You are taken to the Meridia home page (Library).
   `[SCREENSHOT 3: Home page after login]`

## Expected result
You are signed in and see the Meridia home page.

## Good to know
- Without **Keep me signed in**, Meridia signs you out after **60 minutes of inactivity** and shows: "Your session has expired. Please log in again."
- By signing in you agree to Meridia's Terms and Conditions (link at the bottom of the form).

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A red message appears at the top after clicking Sign in | The email or password was not accepted. Re-enter them carefully (check Caps Lock). The message text comes from the server **[CONFIRM exact wording]**. |
| Sign in button is greyed out | Fill in both Email and Password. |
| Forgot your password | Click **Forgotten Password?** under the Sign in button and follow **How to reset your password**. |
